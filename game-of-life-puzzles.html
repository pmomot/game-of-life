Clear

Tap empty cells to add your own, then press Run. Clear every cell off the board.

How to play

  • The board is dirty. Add as few cells of your own as you can, press Run, and let ordinary Life rules do the rest. You win when nothing is left alive — your own cells have to die too.
  • "Fewest possible" is proven, not estimated. A solver searched every placement of one cell, then two, then three, and simulated each one. If a board says it can be cleared with one cell, then one cell really does clear it — and no placement of any single cell elsewhere would have worked any better.
  • The level numbers show ★ when you cleared that board with the fewest possible cells, and ✓ when you cleared it with more.
  • Rock is inert: nothing can live there and you cannot build on it. It blocks and it absorbs.
  • These boards do not wrap, unlike the other two modes. Cells that reach an edge fall off, which is often exactly what you want.
  • It fails when the board settles into something that will never die — a still life or a blinker — or when it is still going after 400 generations.
  • Stuck? Reveal shows a solution, but the level will be marked as revealed rather than solved.
