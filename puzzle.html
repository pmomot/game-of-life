How to play

  • Add cells by tapping empty squares, then press Run and let ordinary Life rules take over. Tap a cell again to take it back.
  • Dirt is the ochre stuff already on the board. You cannot remove it by hand — only Life can.
  • Rock is inert: nothing lives on it and you cannot build there.
  • These boards do not wrap, unlike the other two modes. Cells that reach an edge fall off, which is often exactly what you want.
  • Clear, Hold on and Bloom targets are proven. A solver tried every placement of one cell, then two, then three, and simulated each. When a board says one cell is enough, one cell really is enough — and when it says 55 is the most you can grow it to, nothing does better.
  • Sweep targets are the best known, not a proof: they come from searching gliders, spaceships and other patterns at every position. Beat one and you have found something the search missed.
  • Level numbers show ★ when you matched the target and ✓ when you finished short of it. Stuck? Reveal shows an answer, but the level is then marked as revealed rather than scored.
